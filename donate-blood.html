Eligibility Checklist

  • Age between 18 and 65 years.
  • Weight at least 45 kg.
  • Hemoglobin levels within normal range.
  • Free from transmissible infections and acute illnesses.

Note: Final eligibility is determined by qualified medical professionals and blood bank guidelines prior to donation.
