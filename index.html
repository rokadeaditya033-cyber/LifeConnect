Why Blood Donation Matters

Every 2 seconds someone needs blood. A single donation can be separated into red blood cells, platelets, and plasma, helping multiple patients battling trauma, surgeries, and chronic illnesses across hospitals in Maharashtra.

Read Awareness Guide

Did You Know?

Only 1% of the population donates blood regularly, yet 1 in 3 people will need a blood transfusion in their lifetime. Regular donations also help regulate blood iron levels and support cardiovascular well-being!

Engineering Technology for Grassroots Healthcare

BloodConnect was engineered by student developers at Amrutvahini College of Engineering (AVCOE), Sangamner, under the faculty guidance of Prof. S. S. Dhokchaule. Built to solve critical blood delivery delays in rural and peri-urban Maharashtra by directly connecting regional voluntary donors with emergency patients.
