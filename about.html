Community Engagement Project (CEP)

About BloodConnect

An academic innovation engineered by students of Amrutvahini College of Engineering, Sangamner, dedicated to eradicating emergency blood shortages across Maharashtra.

Project Background & Community Engagement (CEP)

AVCOE Engineering Initiative

BloodConnect was conceptualized, designed, and engineered by engineering students at Amrutvahini College of Engineering, Sangamner, as part of the Community Engagement Project (CEP) curriculum. The initiative was born out of an urgent ground reality: rural sub-district hospitals and primary healthcare centers frequently experience lethal delays in procuring matching blood units during acute trauma emergencies, obstetric hemorrhages, and pediatric surgeries.

While urban tier-1 centers possess extensive blood bank storage, peri-urban and rural towns across Maharashtra—including Sangamner, Shrirampur, Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar), Nashik, and rural Pune—frequently encounter localized blood deficits. BloodConnect leverages a lightweight, community-oriented architecture to establish direct peer-to-peer matching between willing local donors and critical patients, removing third-party middlemen and eliminating bureaucratic delay.

Rural-Urban Parity

Connecting primary healthcare centers and taluka hospitals directly with active regional donor clusters across Maharashtra.

Real-Time Emergency Response

Instant request posting and automated tracking with zero commercialization or middleman commissions.

Ethical & Confidential

Protecting donor privacy while providing seamless contact facilitation once mutual confirmation is reached.
